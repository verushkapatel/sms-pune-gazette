For Admissions 2026–27

St. Mary's School is an Anglo-Indian Private Unaided Minority School with a strong ethos for providing value-based education. Application forms must be filled online. Submission does not guarantee a seat.

Nursery — Boys and Girls

In order to be eligible, your child should have attained a minimum age limit of three years of age, to qualify for entry to the Nursery Class. This is as per the National Education Policy, 2020. Age Band: 01/06/2022–31/05/2023.

Seats: 80 (Girls 40, Boys 40).

Dates for filling the application form online: 15th November 2025 to 15th December 2025 — the link will be active from 10.00 a.m. on the first day to 6.00 p.m. on the last day. Please use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox.

You are requested to fill and submit the application form online. Please read the procedure and other details on the official school site, click I Agree, and proceed.
