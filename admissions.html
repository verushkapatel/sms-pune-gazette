Admissions

St. Mary’s School is an Anglo-Indian private unaided minority school with a strong ethos for providing value-based education. Application forms must be filled online. Submission does not guarantee a seat.

Nursery — boys and girls

To be eligible, your child should have attained a minimum age of three years to qualify for entry to the Nursery class, as per the National Education Policy, 2020. Age band: 01/06/2022 – 31/05/2023.

Seats: 80 — 40 girls and 40 boys.

Dates for filling the form online: 15 November 2025 to 15 December 2025. The link is active from 10.00 a.m. on the first day to 6.00 p.m. on the last. Please use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox.

Read the procedure and other details on the official school site, click “I Agree”, and proceed.

Where to apply

By section

Girls’ Section

Nursery to Class 10. The Prep Section is a haven of learning, sharing and caring. Apply through the Girls’ Section notices on the official website.

Boys’ Section

One section per class since 1992. Individual attention is given to every child. Nursery and Preparatory applications are listed separately on smspune.com.

Junior College (ISC)

Three sections — Science, Commerce and Humanities combinations as published. Timings 8.45 a.m. to 3.05 p.m. Internships and four hours of community service a month are expected.

IB Diploma Programme

Grade 11 (DP1) applications for 2026–27 are open. Limited seats. Eligibility: Grade 10 from any national or international board. Application fee Rs. 5,000, non-refundable. Write to ibdp@smspune.in or telephone +91 93094 83852.
