The hundred and sixtieth year

160 years in Camp, Pune.

Girls’ Section · Boys’ Section · Junior College · IB Diploma

Archdeacon Leigh Lye asked the residents of Poona for a day school for girls, and for five hundred rupees. Mrs Woods was engaged as headmistress. From that circular grew St. Mary’s — run for a century by the Sisters of the Community of St. Mary the Virgin, Wantage, and since 1977 by a lay principalship that has kept the same charge.

A solid academic programme, a child-centred classroom, and an unbeatable campus. The girls’ section, the boys’ section and the co-educational junior college share the same grounds. The IB Diploma Programme is the newest chapter.

A visit to Rashtrapati Bhavan
