Lead story

One hundred and sixty years of St. Mary's.

Camp, Pune · Established 5 November 1866

Archdeacon Leigh Lye and Judge Lloyd asked the residents of Poona for a day school for girls, and for five hundred rupees. Mrs Woods was engaged as headmistress. From that circular grew St. Mary's — run for a century by the Sisters of the Community of St. Mary the Virgin, Wantage, and since 1977 by a lay principalship that has kept the same charge: a solid academic programme, a child-centred classroom, and an unbeatable campus.

Christian principles remain the foundation. Admissions remain open to every community. The girls' section, the boys' section and the co-educational junior college share the same grounds. The IB Diploma Programme is the newest chapter.

Mission

Core values
