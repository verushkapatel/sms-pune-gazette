Beyond the desk

Sports, clubs, excursions and service

The School has played a pivotal role in producing state and national level players in hockey, football, netball and basketball. As of today, though hockey and netball have been discontinued, the School provides facilities for Aerobics, Swimming, Basketball and Football.

Sports

The students participate regularly in both inter and intra-school tournaments. We have prepared students who have played in the national teams for their respective games.

In 2014–2015, Durga Dharmadhikari, Durva Kakade, Saakshi D'Souza, Sai Bhade, Pranava Nande, Saachi Kulkarni, Ali Zainulabedeen Raja and Karl Chari represented the School and the State at the nationals for Basketball. Devika Rajwade, Tushita Yadav, Gia Sundaram, Aishwarya Gadekar, Shivani Harer and Gitanjali Pais represented Football.

Our Under-13 Basketball team won the Late Rajabhau Chitale U-13 District Championship on 11 March 2016, emerging victorious from amongst 51 schools, 44–22 in the final against the Sharp Shooters, under coach Mr. Arun Chopade. The Under-16 Girls football team won the Score Football League on 5 July 2016, defeating Orbis School 2–1 on penalties in the final.

After hours

Clubs

Robotics

The FLL Team, Robolutions, of the St. Mary's Robotics Club, won an award at the First Lego League Tournament, conducted by US First. Two teams — Robofalcons and Nuketech — learnt not only building and code but teamwork, cooperation and inclusion. At the FLL 2017 Animal Allies Challenge in Ahmedabad, Robofalcons received a medal for Best Core Values and Good Code of Conduct. — Arnaav Sareen, 8 D.

Speech & Drama

The Speech and Drama Club, conducted by specialised faculty, offers students a captivating platform to explore the art of expression. Through engaging activities, students enhance their communication skills, boost self-confidence, and develop a flair for storytelling.

Music Group

The School Choir regularly participates at our Speech Day and Graduation Day. The boys also have their own orchestra, which performed live at the Speech Day held in October 2015.

Nature Club

Students of the Girls' Section work together towards comprehending nature, its conservation and sustainability. Awareness is the first aim: painting enlightening scenes, writing one-liners on a t-shirt, making informative bookmarks.

Chess Club

The Chess Club began in 2014. It is held every Saturday in the School library. It improves one's concentration and problem solving ability, and has attracted the girls who like playing the game and want to keep practising.

Writers' Club

The Writers' Club meets twice a week to weave stories and poems. Conducted by children's writer Varsha Seshan, an alumnus of St. Mary's School, the club encourages girls from classes six and seven to express themselves.

Che-Mystery Club

With the help of Dr. Surekha Bhalerao and their dedicated teachers, students of classes six and seven of the Girls' Section prepare natural cosmetics — moisturizer with lemon and orange flavour, and plans for lip balm and scrub.

Student information

Circulars, attendance and academic records are issued through the Edunext Student Portal. Parents are requested to keep their logins current.

Field trips

At St. Mary's, learning extends beyond the classrooms. Students are regularly taken on field trips and excursions to broaden their perspectives and get an idea of the world outside the classrooms.

Girls' Section: Meteorological Department; DSK Institute of Design; farms and fields to learn about irrigation and soil.

Boys' Section: DSK Institute of Design; Morachi Chincholi; Joshi Rail Museum (Preparatory Class); Agriculture College, Pune; the Dinosaur park at the Science Centre, Pimpri; the automobile room in the science park.

Junior College: Indian Institute of Science Education & Research; Reserve Bank of India; DSK Institute of Design.

IT projects

Saturday, 11th Feb 2016 was the day for Celebrating India in all its diversity. 45 boys of Std 4 divided themselves across ten states. Story telling under the iconic Banyan Tree of the Boys' section, an Art Corner, and a food court of the states — Mrs. S. Kulkarni.

On 9 February 2017, Std 5 presented “Globetrotter”. Parents were issued passports filled by their children, visas stamped, and boarding passes for an SMS airways flight Around the World in 80 minutes. Every boy presented, supported by a slideshow.

On 10 February 2016, 11-year-old boys of Std 6 sat SMMUN. The agenda: Prevention of Terrorism in the MENA region, conducted by Aryan Agarwal and Moksh Gadia of Std 9. Eleven countries were represented.

SUPW

Boys of Std 8 visited Prayatna School for differently abled children on 30 January 2017. Art and craft, a cooking session, and dancing to music at the close — an enriching afternoon.

On 30 July 2016, boys of classes 9 and 10 visited the Gurudwara to interact with young adults from Nav Kshitij. After prayer and a play on organ donation, there was a langar where the boys served food to all there.

On 8 October 2016, boys of Classes 7 and 8 visited the Centre of Life Sciences, Health and Medicine at Undri. They met children facing autism and Down's syndrome, put up a short musical show, and came away wiser.
