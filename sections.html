Four sections, one campus

Girls, Boys, College and the Diploma

The school was originally started as an all-girls’ school. It now has a separate section for boys and a junior college, all located within the same campus. The IB Diploma Programme is the newest chapter.

Nursery to Class 10

The Girls’ Section

The Girls’ Section represents the unique brand value of St. Mary’s School. It symbolises an institution where a girl child is helped to develop her individuality based on ethical values and culture. The classes in this section extend from Nursery to Class 10.

The Prep Section, in which the children are gently nurtured, is a haven of learning, sharing and caring. It provides a rich learning environment that kindles the imagination, inspires the intellect and imbibes values in each child.

The Boys’ Section

The unique factor of our Boys’ Section, established in 1992, is that it has only one section per class. This has resulted in a strong bond between students, parents and faculty. Individual attention is given to every child and almost all students are given the opportunity to participate in all activities.

ISC Programme

Junior College

Empowering learners, building leaders, enriching lives. The Junior College was established under the leadership of Mrs Veena Thadani in 2009. Mrs Diamay Menzies serves as the current Vice Principal at SMJC, mentoring and moulding our students into young adults ready to serve their community and country.

Our ISC curriculum is thoughtfully designed to prepare students not only for outstanding examination results but also for success in higher education and life beyond the classroom. At SMJC, we believe learning is more than content; it is an evolving journey that nurtures curiosity, character, and confidence.

To bridge classroom and profession, the Junior College incorporates mandatory summer internships across research laboratories, legal firms, media houses, financial institutions, non-profits, healthcare and entrepreneurial ventures. Students are encouraged to put in a minimum of four hours of community service every month. Euphoria, the cultural festival, draws participation from across the city.

Timings: 8.45 a.m. to 3.05 p.m.

IB World School

The IB Diploma Programme

St. Mary’s School, Pune now offers the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme for Grades 11 and 12. Globally accepted across 75+ countries, the curriculum assesses how students think, not only what they memorise.

Students choose three subjects at Higher Level and three at Standard Level, plus the IB Core: Creativity, Activity and Service; the Extended Essay; and Theory of Knowledge. Executive Director and Head of School IBDP, Sujata Mallic Kumar, has said the introduction of the IBDP marks a new chapter in the school’s mission. Anjali Bhardwaj serves as IBDP Coordinator.

Grade 11 (DP1) applications for 2026–27 are open. Limited seats. Write to ibdp@smspune.in or telephone +91 93094 83852.
